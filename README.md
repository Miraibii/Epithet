**Epithet** is a customizable title and nameplate plugin for Final Fantasy XIV, designed to give players more creative freedom over their character's identity.

Create personalized prefixes, suffixes, and custom titles with advanced color customization, gradients, and animated effects through a modern, intuitive interface.

Epithet focuses on visual customization, ease of use, and character expression, with the goal of providing a flexible and polished experience for the FFXIV community.

**Features:**
- Custom titles, prefixes, and suffixes
- Advanced color customization and gradients
- Animated title effects
- Character-specific profiles and presets
- Modern and customizable user interface
- Free and open-source

**Development Notice:** Epithet is actively being developed. Some features may change or be refined before the official release.

**AI Transparency:** Parts of the codebase were developed with AI assistance, including code generation. Development, testing, and refinement are overseen by the creator.
